# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Albion Online Loot Logger to be a welcoming, constructive, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to feedback.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain disagreements calmly and focus on the project.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Follow the project's licensing and third-party-content rules.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, insults, doxxing, sexualized attention without consent, deliberate intimidation, and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable. Do not post malware, malicious links, credentials, or other harmful content.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or block accounts when necessary to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and may be explained where appropriate.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer through a private channel listed in `SUPPORT.md`, if one is configured. Do not use public issues to disclose sensitive personal information. If no private channel is listed, do not post sensitive details publicly; ask for a private reporting route through the repository's available contact mechanism.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and to public interactions where someone is representing the project.
